### The idea

```text
DATA → INTELLIGENCE → DECISION → ACTION → GROWTH
                 ↘ human oversight ↙
```

We are building toward a **Business Operating System** where business data becomes operational memory, intelligence becomes decision support, and automation turns decisions into action.
